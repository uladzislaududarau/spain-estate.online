2. Data processed

An enquiry includes your name, WhatsApp phone number or Telegram username/number, chosen messenger, language, regions, purchase purpose, property type, budget, purchase timeframe, phone country, enquiry ID, and consent date, time and version. Contacts are not placed in URLs or advertising events. The form does not request passport or payment details.

Cloudflare processes connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website and determine country. The application stores a short-lived HMAC fingerprint rather than the raw IP for rate limiting. Quiz answers are temporarily stored in the tab's sessionStorage; the application does not store your name or contact there.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

Data is used to answer your request, prepare a property selection and contact you through your chosen channel: steps requested before a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and consent under Article 6(1)(a) where applicable. The consent checkbox requires an affirmative action and is not preselected. Abuse prevention relies on the legitimate interest of maintaining security (Article 6(1)(f)). An enquiry is not a subscription to third-party marketing.

4. Storage and recipients

Enquiries travel over HTTPS and are stored encrypted in Cloudflare D1. The operator receives a copy by email delivered by Resend. Each service processes the information needed for its function. Subsequent contact uses your chosen WhatsApp or Telegram. Data is not sold.

Cloudflare, Resend, your chosen messenger and Google when marketing is allowed may process data outside the EEA. Their respective terms apply. The operator must document the processing agreements and international transfer safeguards; this website does not claim that review is already complete.

5. Retention

Property enquiries have a retention period of 180 days from submission. A scheduled task removes expired records from the main D1 database. Rate-limit records are short-lived. Deletion of email notifications and correspondence in your chosen messenger is not automated and requires operator action; D1 cleanup does not delete those copies. Use the contact above to request erasure.

6. Cookies and Google Ads

Google Ads AW-18554200616 loads only after marketing permission. All four Google Consent Mode v2 settings default to denied and Google is not loaded before a choice. Refusal does not prevent quiz use. Category choices are stored locally for up to 180 days and can be changed or withdrawn through Cookie settings in the homepage footer. Withdrawing marketing permission reloads the page. GA4 is not connected.

sessionStorage restores quiz answers. A separate receipt containing only ID, language and time enables the thank-you page for 30 minutes without your name or contact. These records do not provide advertising consent.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, withdraw consent or object where applicable. Withdrawal does not affect previous lawful processing. Send requests to phewcy.dev@gmail.com with the subject Personal Data. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority, including in your country of residence. GDPR conditions and deadlines apply. The quiz does not make purchase decisions or carry out profiling with legal effects.
